# Sprint M3 — Squad following

Source: plan.md §3.1 (succession), §7.4, §9 (M3), §10 (M3 prompt); GDD §0, §1, §2, §14 (fresh
recruits), §15 (approved `unit:died` cause list), §17 Q17; readability.md (leader marker); ADR 0005
(extraction radius rests on the 78 px trail), ADR 0008.
**Done when:** no oscillation, bunching or teleporting, and killing the leader (debug key) promotes
the next wizard cleanly. No combat in this milestone.

Done-when items used below:
- **D1** `typecheck`, `lint`, `test`, `build` all pass. Trail, follow/formation, succession, RNG and
  names logic is pure TypeScript in `src/core` with unit tests.
- **D2** Following holds the GDD §1 verify for 60 s of circles, zig-zags and stop-starts. No two body
  circles overlap by more than 6 px. No follower oscillates or creeps while the leader stands still.
  No follower gets more than 96 px from its slot. No per-frame step is longer than the speed cap
  allows (no teleporting). This is proven by a core simulation test and by the reviewer's automated
  playtest.
- **D3** Succession follows GDD §2. Debug-killing the leader 3× in a row while moving never drops
  control. Held input carries over, the camera eases to the new leader over `successionEaseMs` and
  never jumps, the leader marker moves with a pop, and the trail compacts without snapping. Same-frame
  multi-deaths promote the lowest surviving index. Killing the 4th wizard leaves a cleanly empty squad.
- **D4** Recruits get seeded silly names, unique within the squad. A restart gives fresh recruits
  (seed = base + attempt, GDD Q17), and the same seed and attempt always give the same names.
- **D5** Debug keys kill the leader or a random follower. The overlay shows names, follow targets,
  spacing and the trail. A scene restart doesn't leak bus listeners, keys or sprites.
- **D6** The gate report lists the obstacle-navigation limitations (plan §10 M3 prompt).

## Contract changes (producer, after human approval)

### `src/core/types.ts`: add
```ts
/** Which side a unit fights for (GDD §0). Powder kegs are neutral. */
export type Team = 'squad' | 'enemy' | 'neutral';

/** Why a unit died. Results and Hat Hill share this list (GDD §15, approved). */
export type DeathCause = 'combat' | 'volunteer' | 'left-behind';

/** Breadcrumb-trail following (GDD §1). Distances are in world px. */
export interface TrailTuning {
  readonly breadcrumbSpacingPx: number;
  /** Follower k's slot is k × this far behind the leader, measured along the trail. */
  readonly followerSpacingPx: number;
  readonly separationRadiusPx: number;
  /** Beyond this distance from its slot, a follower moves at squadMoveSpeed × catchUpMultiplier. */
  readonly catchUpDistancePx: number;
  readonly catchUpMultiplier: number;
  /** Inside this distance from its slot, a follower slows down linearly (arrive). */
  readonly arriveRadiusPx: number;
  /** A follower within this distance of a slot at rest stops: no creeping, no jitter. */
  readonly stopRadiusPx: number;
  /** Cap on the separation push speed, as a fraction of squadMoveSpeed. */
  readonly separationMaxSpeedFactor: number;
}

/** Word lists for seeded recruit names, combined as "<first> the <epithet>" (Q3). */
export interface RecruitNameTable {
  readonly firstNames: readonly string[];
  readonly epithets: readonly string[];
}

/** Dev arena squad until M7's mission data supplies the roster. */
export interface DevSquadConfig {
  /** Leader plus followers. */
  readonly size: number;
  /** Base seed. Each restart uses base + attempt (GDD Q17). */
  readonly nameSeed: number;
}
```
`CameraTuning` gains:
```ts
  /** Time for the camera to ease to a newly promoted leader (GDD §2). */

```

## Known limitations to report (D6, expected at planning; final list below)
- The dev arena has no obstacles. Followers walk the breadcrumbs, so they follow the leader's walkable
  path, but separation can push a follower sideways into a blocker. M7 needs the follower position
  resolved against the collision layer (game-dev, M7).
- No path-finding: a follower cut off by a new blocker (none exist in the MVP) has no way around it (GDD §1).
- A wizard far off its slot cuts straight toward it, not along the trail. In open ground that's fine;
  around corners in M7 it may clip a blocker edge.

## D6: obstacle-navigation limitations (final list for the gate)
1. Followers aren't resolved against a collision layer, so separation and sidesteps can push a
   follower into a blocker. M7 needs follower collision.
2. Beyond `catchUpDistancePx` a follower heads straight for its slot, not along the trail, so it may clip
   corners in M7.
3. No path-finding around new blockers (GDD §1, out of scope).
4. Look-ahead follows input, not movement, so the camera leans into walls (ADR 0008, M7).
5. Wall pin: a follower squeezed between the leader and a wall or corner, with a follower ahead blocking
   escape along the wall, can overlap beyond 6 px (2.5% of 240 one-minute walled fuzz runs, worst 18 px)
   and drift up to 130 px from its slot. Needs path planning, or followers blocking the leader (ruled out by
   the GDD). Revisit with M7's maps.
6. Very fast input (direction legs of 150–600 ms) in open ground can still reach 7.5 px of overlap
   (reviewer fuzz). Legs of 300 ms or more stay ≤ 3 px open and ≤ 4.8 px at edges.
7. Under extreme frame-time jitter the overlap fallback can briefly reverse a follower for 3–4 frames
   (reviewer nit). Watch at the playtest.
- Resolved during M3: the edge creep (T6 edge fix) and rapid-reversal overlap (T6 M1).

## Review decisions (CD, 2026-10-15)
- **T6 M1 (fix now):** overlap > 6 px at walls, after succession during turns, and on fast input. core-dev
  lands a deterministic overlap fallback in follow.ts (pick the least-overlapping reachable position;
  tie rule uses `stopRadiusPx`, no new tuning) plus a seeded fuzz test with walls and kills. Prototype:
  worst overlap 15–19 px → ≤ 1.1 px in the dev arena. Remaining D6 item: the leader driving into a corner
  where followers are already pinned can't be relieved without path planning (tiny-arena fuzz 15.5 px).
- **T9 m2 (marker above followers):** the leader marker draws above follower sprites and below the leader,
  so a follower's hat can't hide it when walking north. Stays a ground marker, no new art.
- **T9 m1 (closed):** marker colours are the style-guide gold #f2b42a and ink #1a1020; readability.md aligned.
- **T6 M1 landed (core-dev, awaiting re-review):** tie rule = accept candidates overlapping
  ≤ max(best, `stopRadiusPx`), then take the one closest to the normal step (the "within stopRadius of the best"
  wording let overlap ratchet up). Open ground incl. kills and 300 ms wiggles: ≤ 3 px. All reversal series
  pass D2. 319 tests.
- **D6 residual (replaces "corner trap"):** a follower pinned between the leader and a wall or corner, with
  a follower ahead blocking escape along the wall. Over 240 one-minute walled fuzz runs (frequent walls,
  a kill every 4 s), 2.5% exceed 6 px, worst 18 px. Near walls, slot error can reach 130 px with 300 ms legs
  (GDD §1's 96 px limit applies in open ground). Fixing it fully needs path planning, or letting followers
  block the leader (ruled out by the GDD). Revisit with M7's real maps.
