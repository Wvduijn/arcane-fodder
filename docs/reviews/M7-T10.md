## Verdict: PASS WITH NOTES

The spawner timeline matches GDD §12, and the tests check it at several frame rates:
- **Activation:** at ≤ 400 px, with the activation frame's own dt not counted.
- **Spawning:** door glow at 1.0 s, first grunt at 1.5 s, then every 6 s (the remainder carries).
- **Cap:** at most 4 of its own spawns alive.
- **Tells:** a started tell always spawns.
- **Destruction:** spawning stops, and the step that notices says so once.
- **Overrides:** the map's Tiled properties override the data values.

The producer's design call is implemented and tested: a full hut holds the countdown at the tell,
and a leader leaving the radius pauses the countdown without resetting it
(`hut.test.ts:116-140`).

The structure path is sound. `CombatUnitSpec.structure` puts `structure` on the death record from
both `applyHit` and `killUnit`, and `isStructureDeath` lets the flush emit `hut:destroyed`
instead of `unit:died`. That keeps huts out of the kill count. Huts take Burning and area hits by
footprint. Autos pick them only when no visible unit is in range (`hut.test.ts:248`, plus the
sight case in `collisionSteps.test.ts:418`). The footprint goes on the grid with
`blocksShots: true`, so squad shots hit the hut at its wall and enemy arrows stop there.

## Findings

### Minor

**m1. The default door is never usable as-is** — `hut.ts:99-101` puts the door half a tile
(8 px) below the footprint's south wall. A grunt (r 12) at that point always overlaps the hut, so
`hutSpawnPoint` (`:193-200`) always falls back to `nearestFreeTile`. On m01 that lands one tile
lower and up to 8 px to the side (the row-then-column tie-break). The result is acceptable today,
but the "door" the view will glow (T14) and the spawn point differ by ~16 px, and the API reads
as though the door is used.
Fix: place the default door `footprintPx/2 + spawn body radius` below the centre (the hut-spawn
convention already allows an explicit door), or return the resolved spawn point from `stepHut`
so the view glows where the grunt actually appears. Owner: **core-dev**. Alternatively,
level-designer adds `hut-spawn` objects for both huts.

### Observation

- **o1.** `HUT.bodyRadiusPx` 32 is the inscribed circle. Area hits use the footprint rectangle
  (`area.ts`, GDD §0 table), and projectiles hit via the footprint tiles. The circle only matters
  for runner contact and the projectile body list, where the tile hit comes first anyway.
  Consistent.
