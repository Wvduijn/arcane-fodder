Reviewer: reviewer · Date: 2026-10-22 · Owner of task: game-dev

## Verdict: PASS WITH NOTES

- **Huts.** Each hut is a CombatWorld unit (`hutUnitSpec`) plus a shot-blocking grid solid
  (`hutFootprint`, `blocksShots: true`). The rubble keeps the solid; the sprite swaps to
  `HutRubble`, and the bar and flash stop for rubble (`spriteOf` returns undefined when dead).
- **Spawning.** The hut timeline is stepped from core. Spawns land at the door when it's free;
  otherwise `hutSpawnPoint` finds the nearest free tile, with `isFree` rejecting tiles occupied by
  goblins or wizards.
- **Door glow.** The glow sits exactly on the door point. Its alpha ramps over the tell, and it
  is drawn in front of the hut.
- **Wiring gap (4) is fixed.** `defaultDoor(position, def, ENEMIES[def.spawnEnemy].bodyRadiusPx)`
  (`SpawnerSystem.ts:212`). m01 also has explicit `hut-spawn` doors now.
- **Hut death.** It emits `hut:destroyed` (`CombatSystem.ts:335`), never `unit:died`. The
  listeners are: ConvergenceSystem +10 (`chargePerHutDestroyed`), MissionSystem objectives and
  rune, and NavigationSystem rebuild.
- **Carry-over M7-T11b c1 is resolved.** `areaBodies()` passes `footprintPx` on hut bodies
  (`SpawnerSystem.ts:166-175`), and ConvergenceSystem's barrage picking uses it
  (`ConvergenceSystem.ts:286`). c2: strikes aim at the hut's `position`, which is the footprint
  centre.
- **destroy()** destroys every sprite and glow and clears the huts, groups, triggers and bark.
