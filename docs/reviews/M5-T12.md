## Verdict: PASS WITH NOTES

- **Architecture as Q3 asked.** HudScene is a separate scene with its own unscrolled camera,
  registered after MissionScene so it draws on top. MissionScene launches it with a read-only
  `HudView` and stops it on its own SHUTDOWN.
- **No references kept.** HudModel is a mission-scene system that holds the bus listeners
  (`spell:denied`, `spell:lost`) and copies the slots once per frame. The HUD scene holds no
  listeners and no system references, and stops reading when `alive` goes false.
- **§5b layout.** Slots 32×32 at (8, 320) and (44, 320), a 24×24 icon (12 px placeholder at ×2),
  10×10 Q/E badges overhanging by 2 px.
- **States.** Ready (cream border), cooldown (ash border, ink sweep, whole seconds rounded up,
  150 ms element-colour flash on ready), lost (slate border, dimmed icon, slash, dimmed badge).
- **Animations.** A shake plus a 250 ms fade on `spell:lost`. A never-granted slot starts lost with
  no animation. The denied shake is 3 px, 200 ms, 2 cycles, whole px, slot only, and restarts
  rather than stacking.
- **Clock.** All HUD timing runs on the scene's delta.

### Minor

**m1. The lost icon isn't greyscale.** (owner: game-dev; D7, readability §5b)
§5b says the lost icon is "Greyscale (`ash`/`slate`), 50% alpha". HudScene.ts:245 (`drawIcon`) lerps a
*multiply* tint toward `ash` (`setTint(lerpColor(0xffffff, Palette.ash, fade))`). Multiply keeps the
hue: the lost Fireball reads muddy brown and the lost Lightning keeps a blue-cyan cast (see the
screenshot). The slash and the slate border still carry the state, so this isn't a blocker. But a
coloured lost icon weakens instant read 3 and the grayscale check. Fix, either:
(a) draw greyscale variants of the two placeholder icons in placeholders.ts and swap the texture
when lost (fading with alpha); or
(b) use `TintModes.FILL` with `ash` for a flat silhouette, if game-designer accepts a silhouette as
"greyscale".
(a) matches the spec text.

### Nit

**n1. The badge font is `monospace` 8 px, not the "HUD pixel font".** (owner: game-dev, M8)
Expected for placeholders. Note it for M8's font pass, with the seconds text (10 px).
