## Findings

### Major (observation for the CD, no code defect)

**o1. The mission is about 5× shorter than its target, and the meter is almost never empty.**
An autopilot that just walks the route, stands to fight and taps Q/E won with 0–1 losses in
**about 1:15–1:17** (huts at about 0:24 and about 0:57), against the level doc's 6:00 fast /
9:00 cautious. Kills were 39–44 against the doc's "about 55 enemies plus hut spawns". The meter
was full 9 s in. A human will be slower (looking around, missed spells), but not 5× slower.
Levers belong to level-designer and game-designer: encounter count and density (doc pacing note 1
already says about 55 are needed), hut spawn rate while the squad is near, meter gain (start 40,
+4/kill), and the hut HP vs squad DPS. This is the CD's call after a human playtest. Owner:
**main** → CD, then **level-designer** / **game-designer**.

### Minor

**m1. Portrait phones (390×844) letterbox to a 390×219 strip** (`390x844-*.png`). The HUD and
the results text are about 5 px tall and unreadable. This has been known since M2 (fixed 640×360
FIT, landscape); I'm recording it because the results screen is new text-heavy UI. Touch and
orientation belong to M8/M9. Owner: **main** (carry to M8/M9).

**m2. Results "Lost" column width** — the longest row seen ("Desmond the Lately Alive,
Stormcaller. Left behind.") ends about 20 logical px from the right edge at 640. A longer
generated name with "Fell in battle" could clip. Fix: wrap, or move the epitaph to its own line.
Owner: **game-dev**.

**m3. Debug overlay starts visible under `?debug`** and covers the HUD in screenshots (the HUD
"Huts" label sits under the overlay's first line). Debug-only. No action.

### Observations (for the human; the fun verdict is theirs)

- **o2. A squad straggler is close to impossible.** With the 78 px trail and the 112 px ring, a
  follower only ends up outside on a straight walk-in if something knocks it back or pins it.
  Over 4 entries I saw none. That matches GDD §16.5/§16.8's targets, but the "keep the trail
  together" tension in beat 3.2 never showed up. In practice the left-behind risk comes only
  from Volunteer!.
- **o3. A lone archer outranges a squad that stands still.** The defeat run shows archer #37
  routing round the trees and killing 4 wizards from about 195 px over 30 s while the squad never
  moved. That's working as designed (GDD §12 "push or snipe"). It's worth watching that a new
  player isn't confused by damage from an enemy their autos never shoot at.
- **o4.** In victory runs the surge goblins never caught the squad on the Gallery. It outran
  them at 110 vs 85 px/s, as GDD §13 predicts, so the retreat was a walk rather than a fight.
