## Verdict: PASS WITH NOTES

Every step takes collision as an **optional** input and documents the M7 rule in its header:
- **Leader:** slides along walls.
- **Followers:** keep the breadcrumb rule and slide on their own move.
- **Knockback:** slides, for wizards and goblins alike.
- **Enemies:** clamp, then `moveCircle` from where they stood.
- **Projectiles:** stop on the first shot-blocking tile. A living opposing hut's tile counts as a
  hit on the hut; rubble and terrain just stop the shot, and enemy arrows never hit huts.
- **Fireball:** explodes on the trunk it enters.
- **Volunteer runner:** detonates where its body first touches a blocker or solid.

The accepted calls in the sprint follow-ups (kegs block movement but not shots, archers never
draw at a hidden target, runner-on-blocker detonates) are implemented as written.

**Producer's check: the old sims are bit-identical on an open grid.** Confirmed, beyond what the
suite tests. I copied `tests/core/combat/encounterSim.ts` to a scratch folder and gave it an open
128×96 grid and flow field on the squad step, the enemy step (`collision` + `navigation`), the
autos and the projectiles. I then ran it with and without the grid.

## Findings

### Minor

**m1. Only the squad's open-grid equivalence is locked in a test** —
`tests/core/world/collisionSteps.test.ts:81`. Enemies (with navigation), autos, projectiles,
Fireball and the runner have no "open grid gives the old result" test. My scratch run shows they
are identical today, but nothing stops a later change from drifting.
Fix: add one test that runs `runEncounter` (or a trimmed copy) with an optional open grid and
expects `toEqual` with the grid-less run. This needs a `collision?` option in `encounterSim.ts`;
the scratch diff is 5 one-line insertions. Owner: **core-dev**.

**m2. Spawn scatter over solid kegs** (cross-ref M7-T4-T5 m2). Kegs are now solid, and two m01
spawn groups scatter within reach of one (`grunts-ambush-01` vs `keg-09`; `surge-mid-s-01` vs
`keg-13`/`keg-14`). Core handles this fine: `moveCircle` first pushes a body out of a solid. But
the spawner in game code should place each scattered body with `nearestFreeTile` /
`overlapsSolid` rather than relying on the first frame's push-out, or a goblin pops visibly.
Owner: **game-dev** (T13/T14). Noted here so it isn't lost.

## Addendum (2026-10-22): re-check

- **m1 resolved.** `tests/core/world/openGridEquivalence.test.ts` runs the encounter sim (enemies
  with navigation, autos, projectiles) at 30/60/144 fps, and the M6 sim (Fireball, Kaboom
  knockback, Convergence, volunteer sprint), each with and without an open grid over the dev
  arena, and expects `toEqual`. It guards that the encounter actually clears, so it can't pass on
  an empty fight. This locks in what my scratch run showed.
- **Bug found by that test, and fixed:** `volunteer.ts` treated a wall sweep equal to `reach`
  up to float error as a contact, so on open ground a runner could detonate early
  (`wall < reach`). It now requires `wall < reach − SWEEP_SLACK_PX` (1e-6 px, labelled "not a
  tuning value"). The fix is correct and minimal. This was a real live bug on open floors, and
  the new test proves the case.
- **m2** (spawns on kegs): fixed on the map (M7-T4-T5 addendum); core-dev's keg-scatter test is
  still coming.
