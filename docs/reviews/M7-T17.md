## Verdict: PASS WITH NOTES

- **Results screen (GDD §14).** It shows the header (Victory in gold, Defeat in crimson), then
  `Time mm:ss`, `Kills n` and `Huts n/2`. Below that: a best-time line ("New best time!" or
  "Best mm:ss"), a Saved column (name, class) and a Lost column (name, class and the epitaph from
  the cause: "Fell in battle" / "Volunteered." / "Left behind."). Again works with Enter, R or a
  click anywhere, with a `chosen` guard. The epitaph and class tables are
  `satisfies Record<DeathCause|MageClassId, string>`, so a new cause fails to compile.
- **Restart.** There is one restart path, `restartMission` with `attempt + 1`, used by pause,
  results and debug. It gives fresh recruits (`attemptSeed`, GDD Q17) and skips the intro.
  Results is stopped before the restart. Phaser destroys its keys on shutdown.
- **Hat Hill (Q7).** Combat deaths are written at death, volunteers at detonation (M6),
  left-behind at extraction. Nothing is written after the end. The best time is written on victory
  only.
- **Leak log (`?debug`).** It logs once per run, after 2 settle frames, to `[leak]` and
  `window.__ARCANE_LEAK__`. It counts: bus listeners, keys, game listeners by name, input
  listeners, display objects, tweens, textures, systems, pools (live/created), entities (units,
  enemies, squad, huts, solids) and the HUD/Pause/Results scene state (active, sleeping, children,
  keys).

## Findings

### Minor

**m1. The leak log samples only at frame 2 of each run.** That's enough to catch anything that
survives a restart, which is D6's question. But it doesn't show the state just *before* a restart
(mid-surge or mid-opening), so a leak that the next run's create() cleans up is invisible. For
T18 I add the pre-restart snapshot from the debug hook myself. No change needed.

**m2. Known quirk: attempt 0 has one fewer `blur` game listener** (Phaser KeyboardPlugin; game-dev
report). I compare attempts 1–10 on that field, as agreed.
