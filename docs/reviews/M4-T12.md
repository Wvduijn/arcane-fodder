## Verdict: PASS WITH NOTES

- **Hit flash:** full white, 60 ms, sprite only, via Phaser 4's
  `setTint(0xffffff).setTintMode(TintModes.FILL)`. I checked that `clearTint()` resets the mode to
  MULTIPLY (node_modules/phaser/src/gameobjects/components/Tint.js:154-158), so the sprite gets its
  colours back. The flash isn't counted down on its first frame, so it lasts the full 60 ms at any frame
  rate.
- **Health bars:** 24×3 for wizards and 18×3 for enemies, outlined, shown only below full HP, with a
  blink at ≤ 25 %. The enemy fill is neither red nor gold. All drawn into one Graphics per frame from
  `CombatWorld`, so no state is copied.
- **Death puffs:** pooled, one colour per variant (charred dark grey, electrified cyan-white, blood
  maroon), grey for wizards (Q4).
- **Knockback:** shows through positions, eased (M4-T9: max 1.62 px per frame).

There are two readability observations for the human and the designer, and one rule conflict for the
producer.

## Findings

### Blocker / Major
None.

### Minor

**m1. Hit flashes break readability §1.9's "no more than 3 flashes per second anywhere", if that rule
covers them.** (owner: producer → CD / game-designer; D6)
I counted `unit:damaged` events (each one starts a white flash) in a no-input run: **34 in the fight,
up to 12 within one second.** readability.md §1.8 asks for a white flash on every hit, and §1.9
(photosensitivity) says "no more than 3 flashes per second anywhere". Read literally, the two conflict
in any real fight. I think §1.9 means screen and impact flashes (its other bullets are about full-screen
white and impact radii) and that 48 px sprite flashes are exempt, but that's a design decision, not
mine or game-dev's. Fix: game-designer clarifies §1.9 (for example, "sprite hit flashes (§1.8) are
exempt; the cap counts screen and area flashes"), or the CD asks for a flash cap in the view. No code
change until decided.

### Nit / observations (for the human playtest)

**n1. In single file, a wizard's bar sits on the next follower's face.** (owner: game-designer to decide,
game-dev to implement)
The wizard bar is drawn at feet + 9 px (to clear the 27×12 leader marker), at `Depth.UnitOverlays` above
all units. With 26 px follower spacing and a 46 px tall sprite, the leader's bar lands across the face of
the wizard behind it (`t12-wizard-bar-overlap-3000ms.png`). readability §2 only says "under the sprite".
That's readable, but it's noisy when two or three wizards are hurt. Options: draw the bar at the unit's own
depth plus a little (so the follower in front covers it), or move wizard bars above the hat. That's a
look decision, so it goes to the human and game-designer first.

**n2. The puff tint replaces any earlier tint state** (DeathPuffView.ts:84). The pooled image is reused
with `setTint` each time, which is correct; noted only because a future gib/corpse pool should reset
`tintMode` too.

## Re-review (2026-10-16): wizard bar above the hat

HealthBarView.ts:72 places the wizard bar at feet − hat top − 2 px gap − bar height. The hat tops
are per class (`WIZARD_HAT_TOP_ABOVE_FEET_PX`, placeholders.ts:52-55: 40 / 46 px for the

the leader's bar (54/60) sits just above the Pyromancer hat, both mid-fight
(`M4-screenshots/t12-wizard-bar-above-hat-fight.png`) and in an east–west file
(`t12-wizard-bar-above-hat.png`), clear of every face. Gate: typecheck 0, lint 0, 531 tests.

**Open design question (producer/designer, already flagged by game-dev):** in a north–south file a
*follower's* above-hat bar lands on the face of the wizard 26 px north of it (hats reach 40+ px,
spacing is 26). This is geometry, not a defect in this change. Moving the bar doesn't remove the overlap
in vertical files, it just swaps whose face is covered. Options for the CD: accept it, draw follower
bars at the unit's own depth so the wizard in front covers them, or show bars only on hover or in the
HUD. Verdict for T12: **PASS** (the remaining point is a CD look decision, not a T12 finding).

## Re-review 2 (2026-10-16): wizard bars on the ground layer (CD decision replacing "above the hat")

The above-hat screenshots are removed; the CD superseded that placement. The new spec is readability.md §2:
24×3 at feet + 9 px, on the ground layer below every character, with a verify line. Implementation:
`Depth.GroundBars` = 6, between the marker (5) and units (10) (depths.ts:13). `HealthBarView` keeps one
Graphics for wizard bars at GroundBars and one for goblin bars at UnitOverlays (HealthBarView.ts:47-48).
The blink is unchanged and both Graphics are destroyed on SHUTDOWN. The hat-height constant and the
callback are gone. Gate: typecheck 0, lint 0, `Tests 531 passed`, build OK.

**In game** (fresh code; leader at 48/60, the others full): I counted the bar fill colour #6ee06e per
screen row (2× scale).
- East–west file (`M4-screenshots/t12-ground-bar-east-west.png`): 38 px × 6 rows = 19 × 3 game px, the

- North–south file (`t12-ground-bar-north-south.png`): 6 px × 2 rows = **3 × 1 game px** of the

hide it". If the CD wants it readable in a north–south file, the options are to offset bars sideways per
slot or add a HUD roster with HP. Sent to the
