## Verdict: PASS WITH NOTES

Clean separation: the brain only decides (move direction plus released actions), `enemyMovement`
only moves bodies, and the combat code applies attacks. Both brains run on the shared T2 machine
with all per-goblin state in an immutable `GoblinBrain`, inside the one authoritative `EnemyState`.
HP and alive/dead come from `CombatWorld` through `isAlive`, so there is no second copy. Every
transition, the aggro edge, the draw aim lock, the whiff, separation, yield and the dead-enemy
freeze are tested at 30/60/144 fps where timing matters. Nothing blocks. Two of core-dev's design
choices touch feel and should get a designer nod (n1), and there's one small state-hygiene nit (n2).

## Design choices core-dev asked me to check

1. **Cooldown starts at wind-up/draw start** (period = `cooldownMs`, 900 / 1800). Agree. It
   matches GDD §12's "Three grunts on one Stormcaller kill it in about 2.8 s" (50 HP ÷ (3 × 6 / 0.9 s)
   ≈ 2.5 s plus the approach). Tested: 10 swings in 9 s, 5 arrows in 9 s.
2. **Swing is instantaneous at release, no swing state.** Agree. There's no swing-duration key, and
   the view can play a swing pose off the `melee` action.
3. **Grunts walk to body contact (22), strike within 28.** Agree. The 6 px reach then absorbs
   separation nudges and knockback (6 px) during the wind-up, which is exactly how Q2's whiff gets
   rare but possible.
4. **Archers draw whenever ready and in range, even inside the retreat band.** See n1.
5. **Separation from walked positions, half each, capped; yield as a hard constraint.** Agree. The
   bounds limit is documented and belongs to M7's collision work.

### Nit

**n1. Archer behaviour when pushed is an interpretation; confirm it with the designer.** (owner: game-designer)
goblin.ts:230-237: a ready archer draws as soon as the target is within 220, *including* below
`retreatBelowPx` (120). It stands still for 300 ms and shoots point-blank instead of backing off. GDD
§12 reads "idle → keep range (step back if closer than 120 …) → draw", which supports either order.
Gameplay effect: a squad that pushes into an archer takes one more arrow before the archer starts
retreating. That fits "push or snipe" (the push still wins, because the archer retreats at 70 vs 110),
but it makes pushing a little more costly. GDD §16 request 2 (< 25 % of squad damage from arrows)
will measure it. Ask game-designer to confirm, or add "an archer below 120 px retreats instead of
drawing". If it changes, it's a two-line change in `keep-range`. No code change requested now.

**n2. A dead enemy keeps its last `velocity` and `knockbacks`.** (owner: core-dev)
enemyMovement.ts:171 returns the dead enemy unchanged. Frozen is correct (D4), but a view that
mirrors or animates from `velocity` (T9 sprite flip, a future walk cycle) would show a corpse still
"walking" for the frames until removal. Suggest returning `{ ...e, velocity: ZERO, knockbacks: [] }`
for dead enemies, or documenting that views must check `isAlive` first. Low impact in M4 because
`CombatSystem` removes dead enemies.
