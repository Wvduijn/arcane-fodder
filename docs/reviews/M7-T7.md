## Verdict: PASS WITH NOTES

The design follows CD Q2 and Q3 exactly:
- The field is Dijkstra on tiles, 10/14 costs, with no corner cutting. It is rebuilt by
  `FlowFieldClock` at the data interval (`NAVIGATION.flowFieldRebuildMs` 250), and its
  `invalidate()` lets a keg or hut change force a rebuild.
- An enemy only follows the field when its body can't walk the straight line (`pathClear` with
  its own radius). It only does this while heading toward its target, so archers backing off are
  not affected.
- Sight blocks target selection only: wizard autos, archer retargeting and archer draws. Aggro
  ignores sight ("goblins hear you"). Kegs never block sight, and a hut is seen through its own
  footprint.
- `selectTarget` runs the costly sight test only on a candidate that would win. This is a good
  optimisation and keeps the old tie-break order.

The heap is lazy-deletion on typed arrays, sized for the worst case (≈ 0.9 MB on m01). Building
the field on the real map takes 0.74 ms.

## Findings

### Minor

**m1. `pathClear` runs every frame for every chasing enemy** — `flowField.ts:197`, called from
`enemyMovement.ts:243`. Each call sweeps the whole line to the target in steps of
`min(r, tile)/2` ≈ 5–6 px. On open ground that is ~150 probes per enemy, each testing the 3×3
terrain box plus every dynamic solid. I measured 1.5 ms per frame for 30 enemies on a desktop.
The full surge plus leftovers is about 30 enemies, and on a phone (M9) this could cost several
ms of a 16 ms frame.
Fix (any one): cache the `pathClear` answer per enemy and refresh it on the flow-field clock
tick (250 ms), or with a short per-enemy stagger; or run a cheap DDA `castSegment` first and
sweep only when the centre line is clear. Owner: **core-dev**. Not blocking for M7; I will
measure frame time at T18.

**m2. The field ignores body width** — `flowField.ts:143-148` `canStep` only checks tile
blocking. A 1-tile (16 px) gap is "walkable" to the field but impassable for every body (r 10–12),
so an enemy routed through one would stick. **m01 has no such gap** (scan above), so this does
not matter for M7. Suggest a rule-6-style loader warning for 1-tile gaps in `mapData`
(conventions §3 already requires ≥ 5 tiles where the squad passes), or a clearance-aware field
post-MVP. Owner: **core-dev** (warning) / note for **level-designer**.

**m3. An archer walking back to its post doesn't navigate** — `goblin.ts` `holdPost` sets
`toward(post)`, but `navigate()` (`enemyMovement.ts:241-252`) only steers toward
`brain.target`, which is null while idle. A ledge archer knocked off its post to the far side of
a trunk line (a Kaboom on `keg-07`/`keg-08`) slides along the trunks but can stall on a concave
spot. Low impact, because a Kaboom that close usually kills an 18 HP archer.
Fix: pass the post as the steer target when idle with a post. Owner: **core-dev**. Verify at T18.

## Addendum (2026-10-22): re-check

  (`{ goal, straightClear }`, `enemyMovement.ts:72-79`). It is recomputed only when
