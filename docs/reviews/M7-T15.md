## Verdict: PASS WITH NOTES

- **Runes and portal opening.** Each `mission:target-destroyed` lights the next rune; the last
  starts the opening. `portal:opened` starts the surge and is heard by ConvergenceSystem (+50,
  `chargeOnPortalOpen`), PortalView (burst plus a 100 ms flash through the shared flash budget) and
  MissionHudModel. The ring is drawn once the portal leaves `sealed`.
- **Surge.** Orders come from core `stepSurge` with `wizardPositions` (squad and runners) and
  `isAlive`. Each order is scattered off solids and spawned with `aggro` from the order
  (`'always'`), then counted with `recordSurgeSpawn`.
- **Extraction (wiring gap 1 is fixed).** `checkExtraction` gets `isAlive: this.options.isAlive`
  (`PortalSystem.ts:200-206`) in `lateStep`, after the death flush. `portal:extracted` carries
  saved and left-behind. MissionSystem writes left-behind to Hat Hill (Q7), builds the result and
  ends in victory. Gameplay stops stepping, so enemies freeze.
- **Defeat.** `isDefeat(memberCount, runnerIds.length)` is checked in `lateStep` (M6 carry-over).
- **Deaths and Hat Hill.** Combat deaths are written to Hat Hill at `unit:died` (Q7); volunteers
  are still written by VolunteerSystem; nothing is written after the end.
- **Results timing.** The results launch `RESULTS_DELAY_MS` (1000, accepted named constant) after
  the end, counted from frame dt (Phaser clock). The best time is written on victory only.
- **Kills.** `recordDeath` counts every enemy-team `unit:died`; huts never send one.

## Findings

### Minor

**m1. The screen-flash rectangle is sized once from `scale.gameSize`** (`PortalView.ts`,
constructor). With `Scale.FIT` at a fixed 640×360 logical size that's correct at every window
size. Note it in case M9 changes the scale mode. No action.

**m2. A runner left behind is recorded via `squad.nameOf(id)`.** That works because SquadSystem
keeps names of every recruit of the run. `classOf` throws for an unknown id, so if a runner were
ever missing from the class map, extraction would throw mid-flush. It's safe today; I'm noting
the fragility. Owner: **game-dev**, optional.
