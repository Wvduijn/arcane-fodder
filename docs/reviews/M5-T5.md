Reviewer: reviewer · Date: 2026-10-19 · Owner of task: core-dev

## Verdict: PASS

The caster is pure and never touches the CombatWorld. Impacts come back as data for the caller to
resolve through `resolveAreaEvent` with `teams: ['enemy']`, so spells can't hurt wizards. The cast
validation order matches Q4: no spell → denied (lost, cooldown untouched, state returned by
identity) → no leader → no aim → cooldown (ignored, no shake) → cast. The aim is clamped to 280 px
along the line to the leader. Lightning is a pending strike that resolves on the frame its telegraph
has elapsed and targets by radius at strike time. Fireball reuses `projectile.ts` (swept), with its
range set to the cast distance, explodes at the contact point on the first listed body, and reports
an expiry impact at the clamped aim itself, so it can't drift from float summing (D2). In-flight
casts never consult the squad or the slots (Q4). Cooldowns reuse `cooldown.ts` with its carry, so the
long-run rate is exact at 30/60/144 fps.
