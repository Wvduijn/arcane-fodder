### Nit

**n1. The archer keg is just out of reach at max cast range.** (owner: game-designer; D5, GDD §8 verify 3)
Archers advance until 200 px from the nearest wizard (`advanceAbovePx` 200), and the cast range is
280. So when the leader stops at the very edge of cast range to the keg, the archers settle 80 px in
front of it in x. With the 24 px y offset that puts them 83.5 px from the keg, outside the 82 reach.
They fall inside once the leader is about 2 px closer (dx ≤ 78.4). Pushing in further is safe:
inside 120 px the archers retreat toward or past the keg and stay in reach. In practice this is a
2 px edge, and the lesson ("push in, then Lightning the keg") holds. If the playtest shows misses at
long range, pack the archers closer in y (offset ±16 gives dx ≤ 80.4) rather than moving the keg.
Moving the keg doesn't help, because the 80 px gap comes from cast range minus advance distance.

**n2. GDD §8 verify step (2) assumes the Fireball comes from the east.** (owner: game-designer)
A Fireball explodes on contact 6 + 8 = 14 px from the keg centre, and its keg reach is 44 + 8 = 52.
Fired from the spawn, the contact point is about 63 px from the middle keg, so only the end keg
triggers and the 3-beat cascade holds. Fired along the chain axis (from due south or north), the
contact point is 50 px from the middle keg, and both kegs fuse on the same frame (2 beats). That's
correct behaviour, not a bug, but the verify should say "from the spawn side" so a tester doesn't
report it as one.
