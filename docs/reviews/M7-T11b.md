## Verdict: PASS

- `ringTouches` uses the exact closest point on an axis-aligned square: `max(0, |d| − half)` per
  axis, then `hypot ≤ radius`. A centre inside the footprint gives 0, so it always hits. A body
  without `footprintPx` keeps the old body rule unchanged, so no unit result moves.
- The footprint flows from the world unit (`unitBody`) into both `resolveAreaEvent` and
  `applyAreaStatus`, so every blast hits huts by footprint: Fireball, Lightning, Combust, Kaboom,
  Convergence strikes, the Volunteer detonation, and burning ground. `bodiesInRadius` honours it
  too, for barrage picking.
- Tests: the corner diagonal that the inscribed circle missed now hits, while ±0.5 px outside
  misses. Touching a face exactly hits (≤), and a centre inside the footprint hits. The rule is
  checked across Fireball, Volunteer and Meteor blasts, burning ground and `bodiesInRadius`. Units
  keep the body rule (control).
