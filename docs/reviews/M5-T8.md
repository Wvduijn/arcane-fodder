### Nit

**n1. No reactions-on twin of the "1-HP squad dies one by one" case.** (owner: core-dev)
NEAR_CONTACT covers one goblin-made succession under M5 rules. The M4 1-HP case covers repeated
successions down to the last wizard, but only under M4 rules. A NEAR_CONTACT run with
`wizardMaxHp: [1, 1, 1, 1]` and reactions on would cover chained succession while blasts and
Combust deaths share the frame flush. That's the M5-specific risk.

**n2. The spell sim exercises the 2-beat chain, not GDD §8 verify step (2).** (owner: core-dev)
The script fires at the middle keg (2 beats: middle, then both ends together). GDD §8 verify (2)
asks for a Fireball from the spawn side at the south keg, which gives 3 beats. The abstract 3-keg
test in reactions.test.ts covers the timing, so this is coverage on real data only. If cheap,
add a south-keg shot. I'll play both at the gate either way.
