## Verdict: PASS WITH NOTES

- **One state.** Core `KegState` is the only state; sprites, ring and sparks mirror it.
- **Fuse timing.** The `beforeStep` rule is derived from the game-loop frame. A keg lit in the
  Spell phase skips that frame's step; one lit in Combat or the cascade doesn't. The fuse and ring
  therefore last exactly `fuseMs`, as measured below.
- **Blink.** The fix main asked for has landed: the blink is now **white/crimson** at a 100 ms
  period (KegSystem.ts:31, `[0xffffff, Palette.crimson]`). It never asks the FlashBudget, per the
  CD's exemption.
- **Kaboom FX.** Kaboom asks the budget for its core flash and restarts (never stacks) a 2 px,
  120 ms shake.
- **Combust FX.** A 32 px pop and decorative ember arcs.
- **Pooling.** Everything is pooled.
- **Debug G.** Respawns kegs with fresh ids.
