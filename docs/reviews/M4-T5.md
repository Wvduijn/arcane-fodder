### Nit

**n1. "Valid target" doesn't include "still in range".** (owner: game-dev for T10, core-dev for T7)
`shouldRetarget` only fires early on an invalid target, and the doc defines invalid as "dead, gone,
or none" (selectTarget.ts:10). A target that walks out of range stays selected for up to
`retargetMs` (250 ms wizards, 500 ms goblins). For wizards, `CombatSystem` must not fire at a
current target that is beyond `rangePx` (or pass `targetValid = false` when it is out of range).
Otherwise a wizard can fire a bolt that dies at 1.2 × range without reaching anyone. For grunts this
is intended (chase). It's a caller decision; I'll check it in T7 and T10.
