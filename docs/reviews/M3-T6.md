## Verdict: PASS WITH NOTES (one major to settle before the gate)

The steering is well designed. It uses slot feed-forward plus arrive, so a follower on a moving slot
stays exactly on it. Members ahead have right of way, which avoids the push-pull of two-way
separation. There's an exact zero-velocity rest rule and a provable per-step bound. The module is
pure, deterministic and reads every number from its arguments. The tests reproduce the GDD §1 Verify
as the sprint defines it, at 144, 60 and 30 fps, and they pass. My own fuzzing confirms that envelope:
in open ground with direction legs of 500 ms or more, overlap never exceeded 4.2 px over 20 random
minutes, followers never moved while the leader stood still, and the step cap always held.

Outside that envelope, overlap goes well past 6 px in three realistic situations. Only one of them
(fast input) is documented. Details are in M1. Whether D2's "no two body circles overlap by more than
6 px" must hold in those situations is a design call. Either the CD accepts the documented limitation
before G, or core-dev fixes it.

Diagnosis, from per-seed traces:
- **Edge cases.** Every over-6 episode at 500–1500 ms holds happens with the leader sliding along the
  arena edge (y = 10 or y = 1526) and then turning back along it. The clamp turns a 90° or 135° input
  into an actual reversal, and the followers can't sidestep into the wall. The worst pair is leader
  versus follower 2.
- **Succession.** A controlled grid with one turn, then one leader kill at a delay of 0–1200 ms, stays
  ≤ 4.9 px for 45°, 90° and 180° turns. A 135° turn with the kill 100–200 ms later gives 5.5–7.3 px

  held input carrying it back into its own followers (seed 14: size 2, kill 415 ms after a diagonal

- **Tuning alone doesn't fix it.** Raising `separationMaxSpeedFactor` from 0.5 to 1.5 brings the

## Findings

### Blocker

### Major

**M1. Body overlap exceeds 6 px outside the scripted Verify: at walls, after succession during turns,
and on fast input.** (owner: core-dev for a fix, producer and CD for the acceptance call; D2, D3, D6)
The scripted tests prove D2 for the patterns they script, and my fuzz agrees for open ground with
legs of 500 ms or more. But D2's overlap clause isn't limited to open ground (only the 96 px clause
is), and these situations are routine play:
1. *Walls (undocumented).* Leader sliding along an edge, then turning back: 10–13.5 px even with
   long legs. M7 maps are full of walls, so this gets worse there, not better.
2. *Succession during manoeuvres (undocumented).* 135° turn plus a kill 100–200 ms later: up to
   7.3 px. Turn, kill, then the heir walking back into its followers: 13–17 px. M4 combat kills
   leaders mid-manoeuvre all the time.
3. *Legs shorter than about 500 ms (documented, tested as bounded).* 12.9 px at 300–1000 ms holds,
   17.9 px at 150–600 ms.
Nothing teleports, slot error stays ≤ 54 px, and everything settles, so this is a bunching and
readability problem, not a correctness one.

Likely cause: `yieldTo` (follow.ts:134-162) applies each member-ahead constraint in turn. A sidestep
around member 0 can head straight into member 1 or into a clamped wall, and nothing re-checks the
earlier constraints. Separation then pushes out at only 0.5 S while the leader, who is never pushed,
walks in at 1 S.

Fix options for core-dev, cheapest first:
- (a) After `yieldTo`, re-run the approach check against every member ahead and fall back to the
  non-sidestep slide, or to zero, if the sidestep violates one.
- (b) After integration and the clamp, add a positional overlap resolution against lower indexes,
  limited to the remaining step budget, so the bound at :25 still holds.
- (c) Let separation from the *leader* specifically use the catch-up cap, since the leader can't
  yield.

Add a fuzz-style test: seeded random legs of 500–1500 ms, jittered dt, edges included, the leader
killed every 4 s, with the overlap bound asserted.

If the CD instead accepts the current behaviour, the gate report (D6) must list all three situations
with the measured numbers above, and G's automated run must stay in open ground with legs of 500 ms
or more, as core-dev already suggested.

Code: follow.ts step 5. When the follower's slot is moving and the steered position still overlaps a
member ahead, `leastOverlapping` (:224-255) picks from 18 candidates in a fixed order: the steered
position, standing still, then 16 directions at full step-cap reach, all clamped to `walkable`. The
acceptance threshold is absolute (`max(best, stopRadiusPx)`), so overlap can't ratchet up frame by
frame. It adds no tuning keys and no randomness, and the step bound at :28 still holds because every
candidate is within `stepCapSpeed × dt`. The 16-direction count is an algorithm constant, not tuning.
The known corner and pin limit is documented in the header (:29-32).
