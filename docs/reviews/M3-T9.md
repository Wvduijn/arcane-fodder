# Review M3-T9 — Leader marker and camera succession ease

Reviewer: reviewer · Date: 2026-10-15 · Owner of task: game-dev
Scope: `src/game/entities/LeaderMarker.ts` (new), `src/game/world/CameraController.ts`, `src/game/depths.ts`,

readability.md "Leader vs followers".
Serves: D3.

## Verdict: PASS WITH NOTES

The camera ease works as specified. On `squad:leader-changed`, the gap between the old tracked point
and the new leader becomes an offset that eases to 0 over `tuning.successionEaseMs` (sine in-out). It
reads the tuning, as the producer asked me to verify, and the deadzone and clamp are unchanged. A
second succession mid-ease restarts from the eased point. With no leader the camera holds still. The
marker pops onto the new leader on the same frame and hides on a wipe. Both systems `off()` their
bus listeners. The notes are about how the placeholder marker reads, not about behaviour.

## Findings

### Blocker / Major
None.

### Minor

**m1. The marker colours aren't the style-guide colours.** (owner: game-dev; D3, plus game-designer for
the doc)
LeaderMarker.ts:12-13 use fill `0xe3b341` and outline `0x1a1208`. The style guide reserves gold
`#f2b42a` (assets-src/style-guide.md:123, "leader marker") and ink `#1a1020` (:89, "never pure black").
readability.md:50 gives a third gold, `#f2c12e`.
Fix: game-dev uses `0xf2b42a` / `0x1a1020`. game-designer aligns readability.md:50 with the style
guide, which wins on colour.

**m2. Moving up, the marker is hidden behind follower 1.** (owner: producer, to route to game-designer
or art-director as a readability question; D3)
docs/reviews/M3-screenshots/m3-nodebug-1280.png: at spawn, and whenever the squad walks north,
follower 1 stands 26 px below the leader. Its sprite top (hat) covers the leader's feet, so only a
sliver of the marker shows. Walking east, west or south it reads fine. readability.md says the leader
"must read instantly". This isn't a code defect: the depth order is correct, and the cause is
26 px spacing against a 42-47 px tall sprite. Options to put to the CD at the playtest: draw the
marker above followers but below the leader, or add a small gold pip on the leader's hat brim. The
CD decides; I'm listing it as an observation.

### Nit

**n1. Placeholder timings live in code.** `MARKER.pulsePeriodMs` and `popMs` are readability numbers,
kept in game code under "presentation, not tuning". That's fine for a placeholder M8 replaces. If the
real marker keeps procedural animation, move the two timings to src/data.
