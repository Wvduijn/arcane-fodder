## Findings

### Minor

**m1. `keg-09` sits on the route, against rule 7 and the doc's own §6 header** — `objects.json`
`keg-09` (800,880) is in the Bramble Path B / ravine mouth (doc: Bramble cols 47–77, rows 51–58;
the keg is at tile 50,55). The squad walks over it on the way north. Conventions §6 rule 7 says
"every keg ≥ 96 px from the route centreline", and level doc §6 says "All kegs are at least 112 px
from the route centreline". Beat 2.3 wants exactly this keg there ("detonates under them if the
player turns and aims"), and since M7 it is also solid in a corner the squad turns through. That
is a design choice, but it needs to be written down as an exception.
Fix: add "keg-09 is a deliberate rule-7 exception (beat 2.3)" to doc §6, or move it ≥ 96 px off
the path line into the ravine mouth's side. I will watch it at T18 (does the squad snag on it? do
stray Combusts set it off under the squad?). Owner: **level-designer**.

**m2. Two spawn groups scatter onto solid kegs** — `grunts-ambush-01` (800,860, radius 48) has
`keg-09` 20 px from its centre; `surge-mid-s-01` (1100,500, radius 40) has `keg-13` 20 px away
and `keg-14` within reach. Since kegs are solid, scattered grunts can land on a keg and get pushed
out on their first frame. Either the level-designer shifts the spawn points so `radius + 12 + 8`
clears the keg, or game-dev's spawner places scattered bodies on free tiles (also M7-T8 m2).
Owner: **level-designer** (positions) or **game-dev** (spawner), whichever the producer prefers.

**m3. No `hut-spawn` doors** — neither hut has a `hut-spawn` object, so both use `defaultDoor`,
which never fits a grunt (M7-T10 m1). One `hut-spawn` per hut on the side facing the approach
would also let you choose where the trickle comes from (Hut A: the west, toward the Bramble Path
mouth and `keg-06`). Owner: **level-designer** (optional).
